How to Play
Volume
Alarm volume follows your device's hardware volume buttons — there's no in-app slider. Set it to full blast before you start.
Exit
The EXIT card on the menu closes the app (where the browser allows it) and drops out of full screen.
Changing Blinds
Touch: Swipe right to go up one level, swipe left to go down.
PC: Click the right half of the blinds to go up, left half to go down.
Adjusting the Timer
Touch: Swipe left to add 1 minute, swipe right to subtract 1 minute.
PC: Click the right half of the countdown to add a minute, left half to subtract. Won't go below zero; adjustments near the level maximum may land up to 30 seconds over to stay aligned to the clock.
Blind Schedule
Levels 1–5 (100/200 through 500/1000) run for 15 minutes each, stepping up by +100/+200 per level. After level 5 finishes, a 1-minute Break screen appears (tap to skip early), then you're returned to this menu to pick the next level. Level 6 and up (starting at 600/1200) run for 10 minutes each: 600/1200 through 1000/2000 continue the +100/+200 steps. Levels 11–13 then increment by +500/+1000 per level (1500/3000, 2000/4000, 2500/5000), and levels 14 and up increment by +1000/+2000 per level, from 3000/6000 up to 18000/36000.
Full Screen
Press F11 to enter full screen. Press F11 again to exit.
Payout
The Payout button on the right-hand column opens a cash-count calculator. Enter how many bills of each denomination ($100 / $50 / $20 / $10 / $5) are in the pot; it totals the cash, takes 10% for the house, and splits the rest between 1st, 2nd and 3rd. The Payout split selector offers four presets: 50 / 30 / 20 (the standard ladder), 40 / 40 / 20 (1st and 2nd chop evenly, 3rd keeps 20%), 50 / 25 / 25 (2nd and 3rd chop evenly, 1st keeps 50%), and even split (the prize pool divided equally between a number of players you set — default 3, any whole number — shown as a plain sentence in place of the 1st/2nd/3rd rows; if the counted bills deal into that many equal stacks it lists each player's bills, otherwise it shows which bills to break). It resets to 50 / 30 / 20 each time the sheet opens. Each payout is rounded to the nearest whole $5 so it can be paid in bills; the small difference is added to or taken from the house cut (so the cut can land a little above or below 10%), and the raw pre-round figure is shown in small type next to each place. Under the House cut and each payout it lists which bills to set aside (dealt largest-first, 1st place first; the house keeps whatever is left). Figures update as you type, and the timer keeps running underneath. Press Esc or tap Close to return.
