How to Play
Volume
Alarm volume follows your device's hardware volume buttons — there's no in-app slider. Set it to full blast before you start.
Exit
The EXIT card on the menu closes the app (where the browser allows it) and drops out of full screen.
Changing Blinds
Touch: Swipe right to go up one level, swipe left to go down.
PC: Click the right half of the blinds to go up, left half to go down.
Adjusting the Timer
Touch: Swipe left to add 1 minute, swipe right to subtract 1 minute.
PC: Click the right half of the countdown to add a minute, left half to subtract. Won't go below zero; adjustments near the level maximum may land up to 30 seconds over to stay aligned to the clock.
Blind Schedule
Levels 1–5 (100/200 through 500/1000) run for 15 minutes each, stepping up by +100/+200 per level. After level 5 finishes, a 1-minute Break screen appears (tap to skip early), then you're returned to this menu to pick the next level. Level 6 and up (starting at 600/1200) run for 10 minutes each: 600/1200 through 1000/2000 continue the +100/+200 steps. Levels 11–13 then increment by +500/+1000 per level (1500/3000, 2000/4000, 2500/5000), and levels 14 and up increment by +1000/+2000 per level, from 3000/6000 up to 18000/36000.
Full Screen
Press F11 to enter full screen. Press F11 again to exit.
Payout
The Payout button on the right-hand column opens a cash-count calculator. Enter how many bills of each denomination ($100 / $50 / $20 / $10 / $5) are in the pot; it totals the Prize Pool and takes 10% for the house. The Payout split selector offers Top 3 (50 / 30 / 20), Top 4 (50 / 25 / 15 / 10), and Custom 3–7 Paid, each a fixed number of paid places. A Custom split defaults 1st place to 50%, with the remaining 50% split evenly across the rest, and each place has its own − / + buttons to step its percentage by 1%. Stepping a place redistributes the places below it to evenly split whatever's left (places above are unaffected); stepping the last place instead redistributes the places before it, so the set always sums to 100%. The sheet remembers your last split and percentages — including after closing and reopening the app — and restores them the next time it opens. Each payout is rounded to the nearest whole $5 so it can be paid in bills; the small difference is added to or taken from the house cut (so the cut can land a little above or below 10%), and the raw pre-round figure is shown in small type next to each place. Each payout that the counted bills can cover exactly lists which bills to set aside for it (dealt largest-first); one that can't just shows how much it's short. If any place is short, one bold red line under the Payout split selector states exactly what to do about the first shortfall, e.g. "Break a $20 with a $10 and 2 $5's." The House cut's own bill list only appears once every payout balances, since otherwise some of what's "left over" is really still owed to a shorted place. Figures update as you type, and the timer keeps running underneath. Press Esc or tap Close to return.
